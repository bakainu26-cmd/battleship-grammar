⚓ BATTLESHIP GRAMMAR

How to play

  1. Drag your ships onto My Fleet.
  2. Use the ↔ Horizontal / ↕ Vertical button to change the direction of your ships.
  3. Ships cannot touch each other, even diagonally. Leave at least one empty square between ships.
  4. Place all six ships and click I'm ready to play! to lock your fleet.
  5. During the game, mark 🌊 Miss, 🔥 Hit and 💥 Sunk on both boards.
